**Start as nobody in a living medieval world. Learn techniques, survive pressure, and let your history change what your power becomes.**

A story-first sandbox RPG built around remembered consequences. The world records what you do: who saw it, who heard about it, what changed, and what pressure it created. You learn techniques from manuals, teachers, enemies, ruins, and relics. You gain proficiency by using and training them. Once a form is familiar enough, you can make it your own.

## The Core Fantasy

```text
You learn a plain step technique from a worn manual.
You use it to dodge arrows, circle beasts, escape a burning house,
and close distance on enemies who try to flee.

Proficiency rises through use and practice.

At mastery, the form becomes flexible. You evolve it by bringing in
a reference: another technique, a passage from a manual, a strange book,
a shrine memory, or a line you write yourself.

The system reads that reference alongside your history and proposes
new evolutions that fit the way you actually played.

Later, at a shrine, a breakthrough can change the path underneath it:
your base level, element, bonuses, and the kinds of strange upgrades
that become possible next.
```

Events do not automatically hand out spells. Events become context. Training builds proficiency. Mastery lets the player reshape a technique. Shrines reshape the player.
